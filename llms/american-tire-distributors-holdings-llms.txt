## What ATD does

- Distributes 16,500+ active tire SKUs from 110+ distribution centers with 1,400+ delivery vehicles.
- Delivers over 30.5 million tire units annually to more than 85,000 delivery points in the US and Canada.
- Serves independent tire dealers, automotive service centers, retailers and commercial fleets.
- Runs ATDOnline, a digital ordering and account-management portal for dealers.
- Offers a data-driven tire inventory recommendation engine and next-day delivery.
